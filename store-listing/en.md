## Description

Record a browser tab, window, region, webcam, microphone, or system audio, then shape the result in a focused local editor.

Zovo Recorder keeps capture and editing on your device. Trim unwanted sections, crop the frame, add timed text, boxes, or arrows, and export WebM, GIF, or MP4. Webcam presets make the camera bubble consistent, while automatic chapters mark tab switches so long walkthroughs are easier to navigate.

Highlights:

- Tab, window, desktop, region, webcam, microphone, and system-audio capture
- Local trim, crop, audio, and annotation tools
- Circle, rounded, square, and corner webcam presets
- WebM, GIF, and MP4 export choices
- Automatic tab-switch chapters with TXT/JSON export
- Crash recovery for local recordings
- No ads and no sale of personal data
